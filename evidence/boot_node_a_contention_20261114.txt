--- [3] Empirical Findings: What the Data Does and Does Not Show ---

What the data DOES show:
1. Kernel initialization is invariant across conditions (~350ms to 450ms).
2. Median full boot time: WITH VMM is 7.858 s vs WITHOUT VMM is 10.219 s.
3. Telemetry captures top in-guest services directly: cloud-init, cloud-config, and pollinate accounts for >80% of userspace boot.

What the data DOES NOT show:
1. The data does NOT show that the presence of the idle nested Node B VMM alone causes the severe ~16s boot jumps, since slower runs occurred in both conditions.
2. The data does NOT show host CPU starvation: the host has 12 logical threads, and the idle nested VMM consumes negligible CPU.
3. It cannot prove definitively whether packet jitter or DNS retry latency on the bridge NAT caused pollinate delays without tcpdump.
